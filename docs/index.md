# Geog Digital Tools & Infrastructure Series

A workshop series about digital tools and open research practices for geographers.

---

## Planned workshops

|   | Topic                                                               | Date                                |
|---|---------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| [1](workshop-1/index.md) | [Open-source tools for outreach, teaching and research documentation](workshop-1/index.md) | :material-calendar: **19 June 2026** |
| 2 | Software sharing, testing and reproducibility                       | :material-timer-sand: In preparation  |
| 3 | Data sharing and cloud computing                                    | :material-timer-sand: In preparation  |

!!! info "This is a growing series"
    The direction of future workshops will depend on the uptake and feedback from Workshop 1. Possible topics include deeper dives into specific tools, good software development practices for researchers, reproducibility and open science workflows, or something else entirely — we can decide what's most useful. The [Research Software Engineering team](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/acrc/research-software-engineering/) at JGI is also happy
    to help.

!!! note "About this site"
    This site is itself built with [MkDocs Material](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/) and hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) — the same tools covered in Workshop 1.

## Who is this for?

Anyone is welcome!
